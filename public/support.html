A little help

Save and sign in

Guest practice stays in this browser. Google sign-in backs up completed practice to your account. Use the same account when you return. Assessment drafts stay on the device where you started them.

Install on your home screen

On iPhone, open this address in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Chrome’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen when available. This beta needs an internet connection to load the app, sign in and sync; it does not promise offline installation or background sync.

Something went wrong?

Reload once and check your connection. Avoid clearing browser data if your only save is a guest save. Check account status under You → Settings before switching devices.

Report a bug

Open a GitHub issue with the screen, what you expected and what happened. Include browser/device details. Keep emails, passwords, sign-in URLs and account data out of public reports.

Account and privacy help

Delete your account or hide your public ranking in You → Settings. For private help or data requests, message Wentao on LinkedIn.

About the beta

Practice titles are playful indicators from a short question set, not professional certifications. The question bank is curated; difficulty is still being calibrated with feedback. Free hosting may take a little time to wake after inactivity.
